About Linden

Linden Schaffer is a psychedelic harm reduction educator, consultant and coach, and the founder of Reveal, a harm reduction and education platform for people considering psychedelics for healing and transformation.

She came to this work after seeing people turn to psychedelics without the preparation, safety practices or integration support they needed. For the past six years, her focus has been psychedelics as wellness tools.

Since launching Reveal, she has grown a community of 50,000+, reached 300,000+ livestream attendees in 2026 alone, and coached hundreds of people through preparation and integration. The Reveal Method™, her Five S's framework (Screening, Set, Setting, Support, Sustainability) guides Reveal's courses, coaching and events.

Linden co-founded the Los Angeles Women's Psychedelic Community, served on the Global Psychedelic Society team, and has spoken at Psychedelic Science, the Santa Monica Psychedelic Summit and Talks at Google.

Her roots are in wellness hospitality. As founder of Pravassa, she built preparation and integration practices that supported clients long before they traveled and long after they came home. That work led her to psychedelics. She is the best-selling author of Living Well on the Road, and her work has been featured in Forbes, Fast Company and The New York Times Magazine.

For speaking, consulting or coaching: linden@revealcommunity.world
