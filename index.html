FEATURED IN

People are turning to psychedelics for healing without a wellness toolbox to prepare, stay safe, or integrate what comes up. Too many get hurt by experiences that could have been transformative. For six years my focus has been closing that gap, through Reveal and
The Reveal Method™: my five S's framework of Screening, Set, Setting, Support and Sustainability.

Three ways to work together

1

CONSULTING

For psychedelic companies, retreats, clinics, practitioners and wellness brands that want to grow without cutting corners on safety.

  • Strategic advisor: your industry thought partner on programming, messaging, partnerships and ethical growth.
  • Community & education programs: communities, livestreams, integration cohorts and workshop series that build trust and reduce harm. This is the playbook behind Reveal's 50,000+ community.
  • Producer & host: educational cohorts, panels and programming for events and retreat centers.
2

SPEAKING

Honest, practical talks with no hype and no fear-mongering. Keynotes, panels, workshops, podcasts and livestreams.

  • The Five S's: why preparation is the best harm reduction
  • Beyond the hype: how to vet psychedelic guides and teachers
  • Informed consent: what every practitioner and brand owes participants
  • Women & psychedelics: safety, consent and the power of community

Past stages: Psychedelic Science · Santa Monica Psychedelic Summit · Talks at Google · Global Wellness Institute

3

COACHING

Private 1:1 support whether you're considering your first experience, making sense of one that already happened or have a therapy practice and want a framework to hold a conversation.

Together we look at:

  • Whether this work is safe for you, whether you're ready, and potential risks
  • Your psychological flexibility and stability
  • Tools for managing dysregulation during and after a journey

I don't recommend specific substances, retreat centers or guides. I give you the tools to make empowered choices yourself. Coaching is education and support, not therapy or medical care.
